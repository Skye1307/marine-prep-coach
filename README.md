# 🚢 Marine Prep Coach

**Free IMUCET eligibility checker, personalised study plan generator, and document checklist** — built for aspiring Indian maritime students.

## 🎯 What it does

- **✅ Eligibility Check**: Verify your PCM %, English score, age, and medical fitness against commonly published IMU CET norms.
- **📘 Study Plan**: Get a personalised 4–7 day plan based on your available study hours (adjusts for IMUCET vs. Sponsorship candidates).
- **📋 Document Checklist**: A complete, itemised list of documents you'll need for counselling — flagged for your specific situation.
- **🔐 Private**: Everything runs in your browser. No data sent anywhere.

## ⚠️ Important disclaimer

**This app is indicative only** — it gives a best-guess based on commonly published norms, not an official verdict. Always verify against:
- The current official **IMU CET notification** ([imu.cbexams.com](https://imu.cbexams.com))
- Your category's specific age/score relaxations
- A **DGS-approved doctor** for medical fitness

Rules change year to year. Use this app to *start* your prep, not to make final decisions.

## 💡 How it works

1. **Tell us about yourself**: Enter your PCM %, English %, age, medical status, and study hours.
2. **Get instant feedback**: See your eligibility category (Likely Eligible / Maybe / Likely Not Eligible) + detailed explanation.
3. **Study plan**: A day-by-day breakdown tailored to your situation.
4. **Checklist**: Know exactly what docs to gather.
5. **Copy or share**: Export your plan as plain text to share or save.

## 🛠 Tech stack

- **HTML5 + CSS3 + Vanilla JavaScript** — single-file app, no dependencies.
- **Responsive design** — works great on mobile (480px max-width) and desktop.
- **Client-side only** — no server, no database, no ads, no tracking.
